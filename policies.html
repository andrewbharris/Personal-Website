Privacy policy

Updated September 2026.

What the site collects

This is the personal website of Andrew B. Harris, MD, and it collects nothing about you. There is no contact form, no sign-up and no login, so there is nothing to use, share, sell or keep. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising trackers or tracking pixels.

Basic technical information. Like any website, the servers that deliver these pages receive your IP address and browser details. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages and the typefaces come from Google Fonts. Each service receives what it needs to deliver its content, under its own privacy policy.

Links to other websites

The scheduling button and the reading links go to websites this one does not run, including Endeavor Health. Once you follow a link, that site’s privacy policy applies.

Health information

This website is not a medical channel, so it is not the place for medical records, images or details about your health. Appointments and anything about your own care are handled by Dr. Harris’s practice, which gives patients its own Notice of Privacy Practices covering medical information. That notice is separate from this policy.

Your choices

  • The site does not track visitors over time or across other websites, so it has nothing to change in response to a “Do Not Track” signal.
  • It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
  • It applies to visitors wherever they live, including California residents.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.

Medical disclaimer and terms of use

General information, not medical advice. This website is for general education. It is not medical advice for any particular person and is not a substitute for an evaluation by your own doctor, who knows your history, your examination, and your imaging. Do not delay seeking care, or change a treatment your doctor has recommended, because of something you read here.

No doctor–patient relationship. Reading this site does not create a doctor–patient relationship. Questions about an individual patient’s care belong with the practice rather than here.

Emergencies. If you think you are having a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Accuracy. Pages are written and reviewed by Dr. Harris and cite published research where possible. Medicine changes, and individual results vary. The site may not reflect the most recent evidence on every topic.

Accessibility statement

This site aims to be usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, or magnification. The goal is to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

What has been done

  • Pages use standard headings and structure, and images carry text descriptions.
  • Menus can be opened and closed from the keyboard, and focused links are clearly outlined.
  • Text and background colors are chosen for readable contrast.

Known limitations

  • The downloadable PDF guides and the embedded arthritis guides may not be fully accessible to screen readers.
  • Publications link to journal websites, which are outside this site’s control.

Report a problem

If any part of the site is hard to use, or you would like information in a different format, it can be raised with Dr. Harris’s office.
