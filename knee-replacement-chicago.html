Knee Replacement Surgery in Chicago

Knee replacement surgery in Chicago with Dr. Harris, offering robotic-assisted total and partial knee replacement.

Knee replacement in Chicago is a core part of Dr. Harris's practice. A knee replacement resurfaces the worn ends of the joint with smooth metal and plastic surfaces, relieving arthritis pain while preserving your own muscles and most ligaments.

Learn more about robotic-assisted total and partial knee replacement, or read signs you may need a knee replacement if you are still deciding.

Who is a candidate

  • Knee arthritis (osteoarthritis) that limits stairs, walking, or sleep
  • Arthritis after an old knee injury (post-traumatic)
  • Inflammatory arthritis affecting the knee
  • Pain no longer controlled by therapy, medication, or injections

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Am I a candidate for knee replacement?

Knee replacement is considered when knee arthritis limits daily life despite non-surgical care. Depending on how much of the knee is worn, a partial or total knee replacement may be recommended, based on your examination and imaging.

Does Dr. Harris use robotic surgery?

For many knees, Dr. Harris uses robotic assistance and computer navigation to fine-tune implant position and balance the soft tissues, so the knee feels stable and natural through its range of motion.

Total or partial knee replacement — which do I need?

When wear is limited to one part of the knee, a partial knee replacement preserves the healthy cartilage and ligaments. When arthritis involves most of the joint, a total knee replacement is usually recommended.

How long is knee replacement recovery?

Most patients walk the same day and make the majority of their progress in the first three months, with gains continuing up to a year. Physical therapy is central to the result.
