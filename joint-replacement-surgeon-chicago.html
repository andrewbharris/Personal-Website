Joint Replacement Surgeon in Chicago

Andrew B. Harris, MD is a fellowship-trained joint replacement surgeon in Chicago, focused on hip and knee replacement.

As a joint replacement surgeon in Chicago, Dr. Harris cares for patients with arthritis and other conditions of the hip and knee, from first-time joint replacement to complex and revision surgery. His practice is limited to the hip and knee, which means the operations on this page are the operations he performs day in and day out.

His training includes residency at The Johns Hopkins Hospital and fellowship at the Hospital for Special Surgery, regarded by many as one of the top institutions in the world for training orthopaedic surgeons. He has authored more than 100 peer-reviewed publications.

Areas of focus

  • Hip replacement in Chicago, including the anterior and STAR approaches
  • Knee replacement in Chicago, total and partial, often robotic-assisted
  • Revision joint replacement for implants that have worn or loosened

Dr. Harris sees patients in Skokie, across the North Shore, and in the city of Chicago.

Frequently asked questions

What does a joint replacement surgeon do?

A joint replacement surgeon evaluates hip and knee arthritis and, when non-surgical care is no longer enough, replaces the worn joint surfaces with implants. Dr. Harris focuses specifically on hip and knee replacement, including complex and revision surgery.

Where does Dr. Harris see patients in Chicago?

Dr. Harris sees patients at offices in Skokie and the city of Chicago, and performs surgery on Chicago's North Shore.

Do I need a referral?

Requirements vary by insurance plan. You are welcome to request an appointment directly, and the office can help you understand what your plan needs.

Talk through your hip or knee

If you are dealing with hip or knee arthritis and want to understand your options, you are welcome to get in touch.
