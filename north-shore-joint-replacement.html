Joint Replacement Surgeon for the North Shore

Dr. Harris cares for hip and knee replacement patients across Chicago's North Shore and the northern suburbs, with an office in Lincolnshire.

Dr. Harris is an orthopaedic surgeon specializing in hip and knee replacement. He sees patients from across Chicago's North Shore and the northern suburbs. If you live in the area and are weighing your options, or ready to plan surgery, you don't need to travel into the city to be seen.

The North Shore office is in Lincolnshire, convenient to the surrounding communities, including Vernon Hills, Buffalo Grove, Deerfield, Riverwoods, Long Grove, and the wider Lake County and North Shore area. Wherever you are along the North Shore, you are welcome to be seen here.

Dr. Harris cares for patients needing hip replacement and knee replacement, including revision joint replacement. He also sees patients in Skokie and in the city of Chicago. New to this? Start with patient guides and conditions.
