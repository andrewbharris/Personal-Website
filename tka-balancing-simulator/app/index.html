Force per compartment

Why the force matters

Robotic tensioners commonly apply 70 to 100 N per compartment. Studies have used 25 to 250 N.

Gaps open quickly at first, then more slowly as the force rises, the lateral side more than the medial, most of all in flexion.

Here, 50 to 150 N widens the gaps about 2.5 mm medially and 4 to 5 mm laterally. A plan balanced at one force can be tight or loose at another.

At 50 or 150 N, the final screen also shows the gaps at 80 N.

The ideal force for tensioning ligaments in robotic planning is not agreed on. It still changes the plan: more force opens the same knee further, so the same target gaps need different resections.
