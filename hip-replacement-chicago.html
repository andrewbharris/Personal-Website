Hip Replacement Surgery in Chicago

Hip replacement surgery in Chicago with Dr. Harris, a fellowship-trained hip surgeon offering muscle-sparing anterior and STAR approaches.

Hip replacement in Chicago is a core part of Dr. Harris's practice. A hip replacement removes the worn ball and socket of an arthritic hip and replaces them with implants sized and positioned to recreate your anatomy, one of the most reliable operations in medicine for relieving pain and restoring function.

Learn more about the two approaches Dr. Harris performs: the direct anterior approach and the STAR approach. Not sure it is time yet? Read signs you may need a hip replacement.

Who is a candidate

  • Hip arthritis (osteoarthritis) that limits walking, sleep, or daily life
  • Avascular necrosis of the hip
  • Hip dysplasia or post-traumatic arthritis
  • Pain no longer controlled by therapy, medication, or injections

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Am I a candidate for hip replacement?

Hip replacement is considered when hip arthritis limits daily life despite non-surgical care such as physical therapy, medication, and injections. The decision is individualized to your symptoms, examination, and imaging, not your age alone.

Which hip replacement approach does Dr. Harris use?

Dr. Harris performs the muscle-sparing direct anterior approach for most hips and the STAR (posterior-based) approach when it is the better fit. The choice depends on your anatomy and health history.

How long is recovery after hip replacement?

Most patients walk with assistance the same day, return to most activities within two to three months, and continue improving for up to a year. See the hip replacement recovery guide for a week-by-week timeline.

Is hip replacement done as an outpatient?

For many healthy patients, especially with the anterior approach, hip replacement can be done as a same-day, outpatient procedure with recovery continued at home.
