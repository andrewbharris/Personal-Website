Primary Location

Joint Replacement Surgeon in Skokie

Skokie is Dr. Harris's primary location, where he sees patients and performs hip and knee replacement surgery, on Chicago's North Shore.

Skokie is the center of Dr. Harris's practice, and the primary hospital where he performs hip and knee replacement surgery. It is where most patients are seen and where most operations take place.

The Skokie location serves patients across Chicago's North Shore, including Evanston, Wilmette, Lincolnwood, Morton Grove, and Niles, as well as nearby North Side Chicago neighborhoods. Dr. Harris also sees patients across the North Shore and in the city of Chicago.

Skokie is easily reached from Edens Expressway (I-94) and by CTA Yellow Line and Metra service.
