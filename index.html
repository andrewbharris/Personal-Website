The Practice

Every plan starts with a personal examination and review of your Xrays or MRI

Dr. Andrew Harris cares for patients with arthritis and other conditions of the hip and knee, from first-time joint replacement to complex and revision surgery. His practice centers on anterior approach hip replacement, robotic-assisted knee replacement, and complex "revision" joint replacement procedures needed when earlier implants wear, loosen, or fail.

Anterior Approach Hip Replacement

Robotic-assisted total and partial knee replacement, matched to the specific pattern of arthritis in your knee.
