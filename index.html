Nonoperative Treatment of Hip and Knee Arthritis
Not every hip or knee needs surgery. Activity changes, physical therapy, medication, and injections are often the right first step, and where many patients start.

The Practice

Every plan starts with a personal examination and review of your Xrays or MRI

Dr. Harris cares for patients with arthritis and other conditions of the hip and knee, from first-time joint replacement to complex and revision surgery. His practice centers on anterior approach hip replacement, robotic-assisted knee replacement, and complex "revision" joint replacement procedures needed when earlier implants wear, loosen, or fail.
